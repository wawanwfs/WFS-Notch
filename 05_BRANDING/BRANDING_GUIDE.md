# WFS Notch Identity · v0.2

**Name:** WFS Notch (preferred) · **Tagline:** Your desktop, reimagined.

The primary identity pairs an independently drawn bold W monogram with a shallow upper notch curve and dot. Champagne gold is chosen instead of saturated yellow gold, against a deep graphite/obsidian tile.

| Role | Color |
|---|---|
| Obsidian dark | `#0A0C11` |
| Graphite | `#181D25` |
| Champagne highlight | `#F5E4C9` |
| Warm metallic mid | `#D6BB91` |
| Metallic shadow | `#AA875E` |
| Secondary neutral | `#9CA1A7` |

### Assets

- `wfs-notch-mark.svg`: scalable rounded app icon, used inside the UI.
- `wfs-notch-wordmark.svg` and `.png`: horizontal identity for readmes and launch screens.
- `wfs-notch-symbol-transparent.svg` and `.png`: monogram without a tile; appropriate for large-format compositing.
- `wfs-notch-icon-512.png`: icon PNG for standalone use.
- `WFS_Notch_Logo_Preview.png`: visual preview of the launch identity.
- `02_APP/src-tauri/icons/icon.ico`: Windows bundle icon regenerated from the same vector source.

All logos are new designs in this project. Do not replace the mark with Poni's product graphics.
