Import / export iCalendar (.ics)

Paste .ics text. UTC and IANA TZID event times are resolved to UTC. For floating (timezone-free) timestamps, enter the intended IANA timezone below; WFS will not guess. Timed DTEND or positive DURATION (up to 366 days) and bounded daily/weekly/monthly/yearly RRULE occurrences are imported as individual events. Single-occurrence RECURRENCE-ID overrides are accepted only with a matching original master occurrence and an unchanged explicit timezone/kind; ambiguous DST, range updates, cancellations and complex unsupported rules are rejected atomically. DATE/DTEND all-day events and simple EXDATE/RDATE lists are supported; end dates are exclusive.

Local-only. Unchanged series preserve the source recurrence rule on export; edited or canceled instances export safely as independent events. No cloud sync.
